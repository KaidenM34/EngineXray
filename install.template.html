EngineX-ray

A privacy and security report card for any website: an A–F grade, what the page does with your data, and Live watch to see where your typing goes.

EngineX-ray

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

  1. Drag the button onto your bookmarks bar. (No bar? Cmd+Shift+B.)
  2. Open any website.
  3. Click the bookmark. A panel opens on the report card. Findings has the evidence, Live watch shows what happens while you use the page, and Advanced has the WebAssembly engine tools. Click the bookmark again to hide or show the panel.
Everything runs in your own browser tab and only reads what the page already loaded. Nothing is sent anywhere.
