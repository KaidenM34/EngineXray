EngineX-ray

See what's under the hood of a WebAssembly game or app: engine, exported functions, hidden hooks, storage.

EngineX-ray

  1. Drag the button onto your bookmarks bar. (No bar? Cmd+Shift+B.)
  2. Open a browser game or WebAssembly app and get it running (some only load their engine after you press Play).
  3. Click the bookmark. A panel opens: Overview, Engine file, Strings, Globals, Storage, Console, Report. Click it again to hide or show the panel.
Everything runs in your own browser tab and only reads what the page already loaded. Nothing is sent anywhere.
