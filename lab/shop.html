Outdated shop

A shop page with the kind of problems real sites ship: an old library, a login form that would send your password badly, and an outside "analytics" script that copies what you type.

Planted on this page

  • jQuery 1.12.4 (only its version number), a release with four published XSS and prototype-pollution bugs
  • A login form that submits with GET to a plain http:// address (it never actually submits)
  • No Content Security Policy and no clickjacking protection
  • tracker.js from localhost, another origin than this page, that reads the email and password fields and beacons them to /collect

Try it

  1. Click the EngineX-ray bookmark. The report card already flags the library, the form and the missing protections.
  2. Open Live watch and click Start watching.
  3. Type a made-up email and password below. Live watch shows the tracker reading both fields and sending them to another server.

The fake tracker's beacons will be listed here.
